# Fox clips

Drop one MP4 per move in this folder, named exactly as below. See `../README.md` for the clip format.

- `jab2.mp4`: Jab 2
- `rjab.mp4`: Rapid Jabs
- `ftilt.mp4`: Forward Tilt
- `uaft.mp4`: Forward Tilt (Up)
- `daft.mp4`: Forward Tilt (Down)
- `utilt.mp4`: Up Tilt
- `dtilt.mp4`: Down Tilt
- `dattack.mp4`: Dash Attack
- `fsmash.mp4`: Forward Smash
- `usmash.mp4`: Up Smash
- `dsmash.mp4`: Down Smash
- `nair.mp4`: Neutral Air
- `fair.mp4`: Forward Air
- `bair.mp4`: Back Air
- `uair.mp4`: Up Air
- `dair.mp4`: Down Air
- `neutralb.mp4`: Blaster
- `aneutralb.mp4`: Blaster (Air)
- `sideb.mp4`: Fox Illusion
- `asideb.mp4`: Fox Illusion (Air)
- `upb.mp4`: Fire Fox
- `aupb.mp4`: Fire Fox (Air)
- `downb.mp4`: Reflector

- `grab.mp4`: Grab
- `dashgrab.mp4`: Dash Grab
- `pummel.mp4`: Pummel
- `fthrow.mp4`: Forward Throw
